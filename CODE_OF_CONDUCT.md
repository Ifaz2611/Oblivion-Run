# Code of Conduct

Oblivion Run is a personal project. We want participation to be welcoming,
respectful, and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience,
background, identity, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
  project spaces.
- Discuss ideas and code on their merits; give and receive constructive
  feedback in good faith.
- Assume good intent where reasonable, and resolve disagreements without
  personal attacks.
- Respect other people's time, privacy, and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted
sexual attention, publishing someone else's private information, and
disruptive or abusive conduct are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces to
spam, troll, or deliberately derail constructive discussion.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project discussions and other spaces when someone is
representing or participating in the Oblivion Run community.

Report conduct that violates this policy privately to the repository
maintainers through GitHub rather than escalating it in public. Maintainers
will review reports fairly and may remove content, issue a warning, restrict
participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation. Reports will
be handled as discreetly as practical.
